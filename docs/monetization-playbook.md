# Monetization Playbook - ai-security-redteam-lab

This file is a commercial planning artifact, not a guarantee of revenue. Use it to make the repository legible as a sellable product, pilot, workshop, license, or proof-of-value package.

## Commercial Lane

- **Lane:** B2B AI safety and security
- **Primary buyer:** Security teams and AI product teams shipping model-backed features.
- **Revenue wedge:** Credential-free red-team fixtures for prompt injection, leakage, and unsafe tool pressure.

## Offer Ladder

1. **AI red-team workshop** - pricing hypothesis: $3k-$9k workshop
2. **Prompt-injection regression pack** - pricing hypothesis: $10k-$35k custom policy pack
3. **CI safety gate setup** - pricing hypothesis: $2k-$8k/month regression maintenance

## Acquisition Channels

- Security newsletter demos
- AI governance checklists
- Developer conference lightning talks

## Proof Gates Before Selling

- Scanner tests pass
- Reports are generated
- Unsafe examples are explainable

## Risk Boundaries

- No claim of complete safety
- Customer policies must be scoped
- Handle findings confidentially

## 30/60/90 Day GTM

- **30 days:** Publish 20-case public report
- **60 days:** Create policy customization brief
- **90 days:** Offer fixed-scope red-team sprint

## Metrics That Matter

- Finding severity distribution
- Regression recurrence
- Blocked unsafe pattern coverage

## Positioning Note

Lead with the specific operational pain, then show the runnable proof, then offer a narrow pilot. Do not pitch the repository as a vague app; pitch it as a concrete workflow with evidence, risk boundaries, and a next buying step.
